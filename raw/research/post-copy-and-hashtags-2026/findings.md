# Post copy, titles and hashtags for short vertical video (Reels / TikTok / Shorts) — evidence 2025–2026

- Research date: 2026-09-30 (all URLs accessed 2026-09-30). Gathered by a research subagent; not re-verified in the main thread.
- Scope: the post text (caption/description), the YouTube Shorts title, and hashtags. NOT burned-in subtitles.
- Audience: a small Brazilian creator posting in pt-BR.
- Labels: **[official]** = platform help center, newsroom, creator account, community guidelines, or a named executive. **[data-backed study]** = stated sample and method. **[anecdotal/marketing]** = blogs and guides with no method.
- Caveat: some official pages (TikTok Creator Academy, Instagram Help Center) render as JavaScript shells and could not be fetched directly. They are quoted via secondary sources and marked as such.

## 0. TL;DR

1. Distribution on all three platforms is driven by viewer behaviour (watch time, completion, likes, sends/shares, swipe-away). Text only helps classification and search matching. [official: TikTok 2020 newsroom; Mosseri; YouTube/Todd Sherman 2023]
2. Natural-language keywords in the first caption line, in pt-BR, beat hashtags. [official: Instagram Search blog 2021; TikTok Creator Academy via secondary]
3. Instagram: hard cap of 5 hashtags per post/Reel since 2025-12-18/19. [official: @creators on Threads]
4. YouTube: more than 60 hashtags means all are ignored. Up to 3 "considered most engaging" hashtags appear by the title. [official: YouTube Help]
5. #fyp/#foryou/#viral/#explore: no confirmed effect, and no platform claims a benefit. Mosseri: hashtags don't increase reach. [official for the Instagram part; "TikTok confirmed no hashtag hack" is secondhand only]
6. Engagement bait is penalised: Instagram recommendation guidelines, and TikTok FYF standards (like-for-like is FYF-ineligible). A genuine question or CTA is fine.

## 1. How recommendation and search use post text

### Instagram

- Reels ranking signals, in order: your activity; interaction history with the poster; "Reel information… such as the audio track or visuals in the video, as well as popularity"; creator information. Captions and hashtags are NOT named. [official] https://about.instagram.com/blog/announcements/instagram-ranking-explained
- Search: "The text you enter in the search bar is by far the most important signal for Search." It matches usernames, bios, captions, hashtags and places. Tip: "Put keywords and hashtags in the caption, not the comments." "Accounts that post spam or violate our guidelines may appear lower in search results." [official, Mosseri, 2021-08-25] https://about.instagram.com/blog/announcements/break-down-how-instagram-search-works
- Mosseri, 2025-01-22: the top 3 signals are watch time, likes and sends. Likes weigh more for followers; sends weigh more for non-followers. [official via secondary] https://www.highstyle.ai/insights/instagram-reels-algorithm-2026 ; https://blog.hootsuite.com/instagram-algorithm/
- Since 2025-07-10, public posts from adult professional accounts are indexed by Google/Bing by default, with an opt-out. [official change, reported secondhand] https://ppc.land/instagram-content-becomes-searchable-on-google-starting-july-10/ ; https://birdeye.com/blog/instagram-search-indexing-update/
- Claim that search reads spoken audio, on-screen text, alt text and embeddings: [anecdotal/marketing] https://www.kontentino.com/q-and-a/instagram-search-seo/ ; https://www.phable.io/phable-labs/instagram-hashtags-keywords-discovery

### TikTok

- For You uses interactions; "video information… details like captions, sounds, and hashtags"; and device/account settings incl. "your language preference, country setting" (lower weight). Completion is a strong signal. Follower count and past virality are not direct factors. [official, 2020-06-18] https://newsroom.tiktok.com/en-us/how-tiktok-recommends-videos-for-you
- Creator Academy: tiny or off-screen keyword text is a myth and "can get your video flagged for spam". Add only relevant text. Use Creator Search Insights. [official via secondary; the page didn't render] https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/article/Getting-discovered-with-Search
- Claim that TikTok reads captions, on-screen text and speech for search: [anecdotal/marketing] https://almcorp.com/blog/tiktok-seo/ ; https://embedsocial.com/blog/tiktok-seo/

### YouTube Shorts

- Swipe feed, so click-through rate matters little; the question is watch vs swipe. "The audience is the algorithm." Explore-then-exploit. There is no magic formula for hashtags. [official, Todd Sherman, 2023, via press] https://www.searchenginejournal.com/youtube-explains-how-shorts-algorithm-works/494953/ ; https://www.etcentric.org/youtube-unravels-shorts-demystifying-discovery-algorithm/
- Hashtags are optional; useful for real-world events or topics. [official, Sherman, 2023-08-24] https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/youtube-advice-for-shorts-creators-hashtags-algorithm/691835/
- Descriptions help search; limit 5,000 chars. The page covers videos generally. [official] https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12948449?hl=en
- "A keyword-rich title beats ten hashtags." [anecdotal/marketing]

## 2. Hashtags

### Instagram

- Cap of 5. @creators, 2025-12-18: "New hashtag guidance: Starting today, Instagram will allow up to 5 hashtags in a reel or post." Rationale: "using fewer (up to 5) more targeted hashtags, rather than many generic ones, can improve both your content's performance and people's experience on Instagram." [official] https://www.threads.com/@creators/post/DSalXGPCWM4/new-hashtag-guidance-starting-today-instagram-will-allow-up-to-hashtags-in-a ; https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-implements-new-limits-on-hashtag-use/808309/
- Above the cap, the post is either blocked or the extras are dropped; reportedly caption + comments are counted together. [anecdotal, unverified] https://creatorlanehq.com/blog/instagram-5-hashtag-limit-2026
- A cap of 3 was tested. [anecdotal] https://www.gsmarena.com/newscomm-70514.php
- Mosseri (2022/2024/2025): hashtags "do not improve your reach", but they are useful for search. [official via press] https://www.digitalinformationworld.com/2025/02/adam-mosseri-declares-hashtags-useless.html
- Following a hashtag was removed in Dec 2024. [anecdotal, widely reported]
- Metricool 2026 study (24.36M posts, 375,118 accounts, Jan–Feb 2025 vs 2026, published 2026-06-16): posts with ≥1 hashtag had −31.70% views and −33.89% interactions vs average. Correlational. [data-backed] https://metricool.com/press-release-instagram-study-2026/
- Restricted/banned hashtags: these can be unsearchable or have "Recent posts… hidden". The list is not published. [official via secondary] https://help.instagram.com/485240378261318

### TikTok

- No separate hashtag-count limit. The caption limit is 4,000 chars (was 2,200). [anecdotal, consistent] https://typecount.com/blog/tiktok-caption-character-limit
- Hashtags are one of the "video information" signals. [official, 2020]
- #fyp: no official statement that it helps. The "TikTok confirmed no hashtag hacks" line has no primary source found. [anecdotal] https://hashtagtools.io/blog/tiktok-hashtags-fyp-viral-guide-2026 ; https://www.socialpilot.co/blog/tiktok-algorithm
- 3–6 relevant hashtags, broad + niche. [anecdotal] https://metricool.com/tiktok-hashtags/

### YouTube

- "If a video or playlist has more than 60 hashtags, we'll ignore each hashtag on that content." Such content may be removed from uploads or search. "The more tags you add, the less relevant they become." No misleading or unrelated hashtags. [official] https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6390658?hl=en
- "up to three hashtags that are considered most engaging will appear by your video title". This is not "the first three". [official, same page]
- "More than 15 → all ignored" is stale older guidance; the current number is 60.
- #shorts is optional. [anecdotal; consistent with Sherman]

## 3. Caption length and structure

| Platform | Hard limit | Visible before cutoff | Label |
|---|---|---|---|
| Instagram | 2,200 | ~125 in feed; ~55–60 on the Reels tab | anecdotal |
| TikTok | 4,000 | ~100–150 | anecdotal |
| YouTube Shorts | title 100; description 5,000 | title ~40 in feed | description [official]; rest anecdotal |

- Front-load the hook + keyword before the cutoff. [anecdotal; consistent with official text-match]
- Metricool 2026: questions +36.70% comments; comment-focused CTAs +202.78% comments. Correlational, and about comments, not reach. Genuine question = fine; formulaic bait = penalised. [data-backed]
- TikTok caption-length figures contradict each other (100–150 chars +12% vs <100 chars +21%). Unreliable. https://www.thekeyword.co/news/tiktok-engagement-rate-benchmark-2026 ; https://insights.ttsvibes.com/tiktok-caption-length-impact-on-engagement/
- Emojis and line breaks: no credible data.

## 4. YouTube Shorts specifics

- Title = human hook + keyword, ~40 chars visible. No hashtags in the title by default. [anecdotal]
- Description = 2–3 relevant hashtags + one context sentence. [official display rule]
- More than 60 hashtags = all ignored; description 5,000; title 100.
- The spam policy forbids "maliciously misleading titles, thumbnails, descriptions" and "offering rewards in exchange for likes, views, or subscribers". Tag stuffing in the description is spam. [official] https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2801973?hl=en ; https://www.searchenginejournal.com/youtube-video-spam/434524/
- Shorts description links are not clickable (2023). https://www.phonearena.com/news/youtube-shorts-blocks-clickable-links_id149677

## 5. Portuguese / Brazil

- TikTok uses language and country settings [official], and search matches the literal query [official, Instagram]. Inference: keywords and hashtags should be in pt-BR, spelled the way people type them (often without accents). Check the in-app suggestions.
- Brazilian listicles push English #fyp/#viral/#trending/#explorepage with no evidence. These may attach the video to a non-BR audience. [anecdotal] https://www.tiktok.com/discover/melhores-hashtags-para-2025 ; https://best-hashtags.com/hashtag/tiktokbrasil/
- Use English only when the topic itself is searched in English (#skincare, #chatgpt). [inference]
- No Brazil-specific rule or study was found.

## 6. NEVER do

- Engagement bait ("comenta SIM", "curte se concorda", "marca 3 amigos", vote-by-reaction, fake "part 2").
  - Instagram recommendation guidelines exclude "clickbait, engagement bait, or which promotes a contest or giveaway". https://help.instagram.com/313829416281232/ , quoted via https://www.threads.com/@buffer/post/DadeFZxiT5S/
  - Meta definition: "Posts that explicitly request engagement (such as votes, shares, comments, tags, likes, or other reactions)". This page is Facebook-scoped. https://transparency.meta.com/features/approach-to-ranking/content-distribution-guidelines/engagement-bait/
  - TikTok Community Guidelines (released 2025-08-14, effective 2025-09-13): "'Like-for-like' promises" and "False incentives for gifting or following" are FYF-ineligible. https://github.com/OpenTermsArchive/contrib-versions/blob/main/TikTok/Community%20Guidelines.md
  - YouTube: rewards for engagement. [official]
- Rewards or giveaways for engagement. [official]
- Misleading or clickbait claims, invented numbers, fake urgency. YouTube spam policy; Instagram clickbait. [official]
- Hashtag stuffing or irrelevant trending tags. More than 5 on Instagram. Many on YouTube. YouTube 6390658; Instagram Search "spam… lower". [official]
- Keyword stuffing, or invisible/off-screen text. TikTok Creator Academy. [official via secondary]
- Restricted/banned hashtags. A human must check each in the app. Instagram 485240378261318.
- Hashtags or wording that harass, sexualise, or use slurs. [official]
- Wording that makes original content look like a repost ("créditos: @x"). TikTok marks watermark/unoriginal reuploads FYF-ineligible. Instagram penalises aggregator reposting; per press this was extended to photos and carousels on 2026-04-30. https://www.engadget.com/2160560/instagrams-recommendation-algorithm-will-penalize-unoriginal-photo-and-carousel-posts/
- #fyp/#foryou/#viral/#explore/#reels filler.

## 9. Derived agent rules

1. Line 1 = hook + main keyword in pt-BR: ≤ ~100 chars on Instagram/TikTok; Shorts title ≤ ~40 chars visible, 100 max.
2. 1–3 honest context sentences that reuse the words people search for.
3. At most one genuine question or CTA tied to the content. Never ask for likes, shares, tags, specific comment words or votes, and never promise rewards.
4. Hashtags: Instagram 3–5 (max 5); TikTok 3–5; Shorts 2–3 in the description, none in the title. Specific and in pt-BR. Never #fyp/#foryou/#viral/#explore/#reels/#trending or unrelated tags.
5. Take every claim from the transcript; never invent facts or numbers.
6. Give the person a "check before posting" note: look each hashtag up in the app and confirm the current limits.
7. Stamp every limit with `verified: YYYY-MM-DD` + a source URL; treat anything older than ~6 months as stale.

## What was built
The Kit de marca has an optional `plataformas` field: a non-empty list, without repeats, of `reels`, `tiktok`, `shorts`. The existing Kit validation (`estado`, `aprovar-kit`, `editar-kit`) checks it. A Kit without the field is valid and reads as all three (`plataformasDoKit`). New Projetos get all three from `kit-padrao.json`. The Projeto Grilling asks which apps she posts on (after the topic catalogue, no briefing section) and the Kit summary states them in pt-BR. `editar-kit` changes the field and now returns the effective `plataformas`; Vídeos in progress keep their Kit as before.

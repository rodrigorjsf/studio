## Decisions & deviations
- Scope: the ticket lists 11 helpers; I renamed every non-glossary exported identifier of the three `_shared` files (spec: "every non-glossary Portuguese identifier in the template's shared code"), incl. types `Font`, `Word`, `KitProps`, `EdicaoProps`, `EdicaoWithCaptionsProps`.
- `Edicao`, `Gerado`, `Kit`, `Formato` kept (ticket names them as glossary terms, though CONTEXT.md has no Edição/Gerado entries; names like `EDICAO_FPS` keep `Edicao` for that reason).
- Composition id `EdicaoComLegendas` (Root.tsx, renders, tests, docs) is unchanged: it is an id, not a helper; only the component/type got English names.
- Props a composition receives (`projeto`, `formato`, `video`, `master`, `duracao`, `sobreposicao`, `legenda`, `palavras`) and the `kit.json` field names are a data contract with generated per-Vídeo code and were not renamed.
- Private names (`POSICAO_DA_LEGENDA`, `CLIPE`) renamed with no alias (not exported).
- qc.mjs's module-private `FPS_DA_EDICAO` renamed to `EDICAO_FPS` (outside the template, needed by the acceptance "old names only as aliases"); no alias.
- Wiki and `docs/developer-guide.md` updated (wiki-sync rule; the guide had a note pointing at #26).
- `node_modules` in the worktree was a temporary symlink to MAIN_REPO's, removed before finishing.

## What was built
- New skill `/estudio:plano` (`plugin/estudio/skills/plano/SKILL.md`) for a Vídeo in `Planejamento`. The Diretor:
  - hands the Vídeo to the Roteirista-estrategista, who drafts `plano.json`;
  - checks the Plano;
  - has the Diretor de arte render 2–3 labelled Quadros de estilo;
  - holds one Gate for the Plano and the Quadros when the Kit defines the style, and two Gates (Plano, then Quadros) when it does not.

  Her requests are recorded in `pedidosDela` and win over the editorial repertoire.
- New personas, both `claude-opus-5-5` at effort `medium`, with tools Bash/Read/Write:
  - `plugin/estudio/agents/roteirista-estrategista.md` writes the Plano and self-checks it with `plano`;
  - `plugin/estudio/agents/diretor-de-arte.md` renders the Quadros with Remotion and records them in `plano.json`.
- New CLI commands, in `plugin/estudio/scripts/lib/plano.mjs`:
  - `plano` (read-only) checks the Plano against `palavras.json`, `zona-do-rosto.json`, `prints/` and the Kit the Vídeo follows. It returns `pronto`, `problemas.{plano,quadros}`, `gate: unico|separado`, `lacunasDoKit` and `rostoNaoVerificado`.
  - `aprovar-plano <plano|quadros|plano-e-quadros>` enforces the Gate shape. On approval it stamps the Plano, closes the Gate and adds 1 to `gates`. Once both parts are approved, the Vídeo moves to `Construção`.
- `registrar-video` now also records `gate: "aberto" | null`.
- `estado` reports `plano` (`ausente`/`rascunho`/`aprovado`) and `quadros` (`ausentes`/`rascunho`/`aprovados`) for each Vídeo, and flags a malformed `plano.json`.
- New template composition `QuadroDeEstilo` (`plugin/estudio/template/src/quadro/QuadroDeEstilo.tsx`, registered in the "Quadros" folder). It draws her Master (`<Video>` from `@remotion/media`) at a chosen frame, with the Kit's title and caption on top.
- `carregarKit`/`calcularMetadadosDoKit` now follow the Vídeo's own `kit.json` when the prop `video` is set.

## Decisions & deviations
- **The Plano is `videos/<v>/plano.json`,** machine-readable with pt-BR keys, instead of upstream's `plano.md`. The Diretor presents it in pt-BR.
  - Triggers reference `palavras.json` by index plus word text. The text is normalized by NFC, lowercase, and punctuation stripped at its edges.
  - A scene must start at or after `s - 0.001`.
- **"Kit defines the style"** means the Vídeo's effective Kit (its own snapshot, else the Projeto's) has no empty text field outside `som.*`. This is documented in `kit-schema.md`. It deliberately does not read the briefing prose.
- **The face check covers only full-camera scenes** (`camera`, `camera-motion`). Scenes that move or scale the camera are listed in `rostoNaoVerificado`, and the Diretor de arte checks them on the Quadros. Full-screen scenes (`cena`, `broll`) cannot cover the face.
- **Área livre** is checked only for 9:16 and only for text elements: y from 250/1920 to 1−420/1920, as in editorial-direction §7. No side margins were invented.
- **The new main-thread skill `plano`** goes beyond the spec's six-skill list. Its commands need `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}`, which is substituted only in SKILL.md files.
- **On final approval the Status moves to `Construção`** so that #11 has a clean pickup. `aprovar-plano` counts one Gate per approval.
- **One Quadro per label,** at a `tempo` of the Master. With the split Gate, the Quadros propose options for the open (empty) look fields.
- **The H.264 color tolerance in the test is ±24 per channel,** because of the limited-range YUV round trip.

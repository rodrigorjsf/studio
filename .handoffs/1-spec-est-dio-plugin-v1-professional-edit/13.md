## Decisions & deviations
- **The cost** of a turn is the sum of `total_tokens` and `duration_ms` (as seconds) that the Agent tool reports for each persona run in that turn: the three Críticos, plus the Motion designer's fix when there is one. The Diretor passes it in. Nível 2 credits are already recorded by `gastar-creditos`.
- **The cap semantics:** 3 rejected turns in a row per cycle. Escalation opens the Gate, and `qc-interno` refuses turns while any Gate is open (`awaiting-criadora`). When she gives a direction, the Diretor closes the Gate and a new cycle of 3 turns starts (`ciclo` 2). This reset is my reading of user story 63; the spec does not state it.
- **The escalation question** counts in `perguntas` (through `registrar-video`). It is not counted in `gates`: it is a question inside the build, not an approval Gate. Option 1 ("see it as it is") is `abrir-revisao`, which is allowed after an escalated turn.
- **One turn = all three Críticos.** Every turn must hold a verdict from each of the three. An approved version cannot get another turn (`already-approved`).
- **A damaged `qc-interno.json`** is refused with `invalid-turns` in both commands. It never reads as a fresh start, which would lift the cap.
- **New counters:** `turnosInternos`, `tokensInternos` and `segundosInternos` are regular `COUNTERS`. They start at 0 in new Vídeos, `estado` validates them, and `registrar-video` can `somar` them. The Quadros and Entrega loops record through `somar`.
- **Scope absorbed:** a Crítico review of the Quadros de estilo. The plano skill had a pending note assigning "the internal review of the Plano by the Críticos" to #13. `plano.json` itself stays held by the deterministic `plano` check; the Críticos judge what can be seen.
- **Área livre** for 16:9 and 1:1 is my choice (outer 5% of the frame). The spec defines Área livre only for the vertical frame.
- **The Críticos keep Bash**, following the #12 precedent (`check-plugin` allows it). They sample frames only into a `mktemp -d` folder outside the Estúdio.

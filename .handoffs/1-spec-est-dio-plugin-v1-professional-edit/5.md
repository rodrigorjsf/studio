## What was built
- New skill `/estudio:perfil` (`plugin/estudio/skills/perfil/SKILL.md`), played by the Entrevistador. It runs the Perfil Grilling: seven questions, asked once, over at most two `AskUserQuestion` screens (or one text question at a time):
  - `quem-sou`, `trabalho`, `rotina`, `tempo-para-aprovar` (free text);
  - `nivel-tecnico`, `tom`, `autonomia` (choices).
- It writes `perfil.md` in pt-BR at the Estúdio root, then runs `estado` until no error names that file.
- Every question offers "Decide você (recomendo: X)". The recommendation becomes the answer, and the question's key is added to the frontmatter list `decide-voce`.
- On an existing Perfil, the skill switches to edit mode. It shows the current answers, marking those "decidido por mim", and asks which to change. It re-asks only those and updates them in both the frontmatter and the body.
  - A key picked with "decide você" is added to `decide-voce`.
  - A key she answers herself is removed from it.
- `/estudio:estudio` now runs the Perfil Grilling when `nextStep.action` is `perfil`, and adapts its tone to `perfil.tom` and `perfil.nivelTecnico`.
- `estado` validates the Perfil and reports `perfil.{present, autonomia, tom, nivelTecnico, decideVoce}`.

## Decisions & deviations
- **The Perfil schema is the frontmatter:** one key per question, ASCII keys, pt-BR values. It lives in `plugin/estudio/scripts/lib/perfil.mjs`.
  - Choice values are matched ignoring accents and case (`media` → `média`), including Mac NFD text, and reported canonically.
  - An invalid choice is reported as `null`. Act on `perfil.*` only when `errors` is empty (`nextStep` is `corrigir-erros` otherwise).
  - The body is free pt-BR prose. It is not validated.
- **"Decide você" is recorded as a key list, `decide-voce`.** The recommended value itself becomes the answer.
- **Recommendations:**
  - free-text questions: what she already said in the session, else a neutral default;
  - `nivel-tecnico`: iniciante;
  - `tom`: explicar mais;
  - `autonomia`: média.
- **The skill describes Autonomia to her in plain words**, because she has to understand the choice she is making:
  - baixa: asks at every Gate;
  - média: auto-approves what the Kit already answers, and the plano, custo and final review still ask her;
  - alta: decides almost everything and stops only for the review before the final video;
  - at every level, credit spending always waits for her.
  - The Spec reviewer called this mapping creep. It is kept, but the Gate primitive ticket must honour it or change the skill's wording.
- **The entry skill runs the Perfil skill** through `estudio:perfil`, with a relative link fallback (`../perfil/SKILL.md`, which stays inside the package).
- The `estado` output gained `perfil.nivelTecnico`, used by the Director's stance to decide how much to explain.

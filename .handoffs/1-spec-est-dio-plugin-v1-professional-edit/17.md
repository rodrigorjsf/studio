## What was built
- **Automatic approvals.** New CLI command `aprovar-automatico "<folder>" "<projeto>" "<vídeo>" "<gate>"` (`plugin/estudio/scripts/lib/autonomia.mjs`). With Autonomia `alta`, the Diretor approves the Plano and Quadros de estilo Gates (`plano`, `quadros`, `plano-e-quadros`) on her behalf.
  - The approval runs through `aprovar-plano`, so the same rule checks apply.
  - It counts the Gate and adds 1 to `aprovacoesAutomaticas`.
  - It lists `{gate, autonomia, em}` in the Vídeo's `aprovacoes-automaticas.json`. `estado` reports that list for each Vídeo.
  - The plano skill (step 4) tells her in one line every time.
- **Metrics.** `estado` now reports `metricas` for each Projeto: `{videos, medias, porVideo}`.
  - They cover the delivered Vídeos (Entregue or Arquivado) that have both `iniciadoEm` and `entregueEm`.
  - Each covers `perguntas`, `gates`, `aprovacoesAutomaticas`, `rodadas`, `minutosAteEntrega` and the #13 internal-loop counters. Averages are rounded to one decimal.
  - `porVideo` lists the Vídeos in the order they were started. The projetos skill shows the averages.
- **Kit learnings and the archive.**
  - After the Entrega, edicao step 6 has the Diretor propose at most 3 Kit learnings and ask her once.
  - The new `arquivar "<folder>" "<projeto>" "<vídeo>" '<json>'` (`lib/arquivar.mjs`) applies only the learnings she approved, through `editar-kit`: the Kit is re-validated, and existing Vídeos keep their Kit.
  - It records every proposal in `aprendizados.json`, sets Status Arquivado and records `arquivadoEm`.
  - `estado.aprendizadosPendentes` lists delivered Vídeos whose learnings she has not answered yet.

## Decisions & deviations
- **Eligibility by Autonomia.**
  - `alta` auto-approves only the Plano and Quadros Gates.
  - `média` and `baixa` auto-approve no Gate. Média's "what the Kit already answers" happens before any Gate: the short briefing, and the single merged Gate when the Kit defines the style.
  - Credits, Higgsedit, the Pré-corte, the Kit, her review of the edit and the learnings return `never-automatic`. The Perfil says alta "stops only to see the video before the final", and credits always wait for her.
  - The Perfil copy was rewritten to match (média no longer promises "deixo registrado").
- **Automatic approvals in Nível 2.** They also apply there. The credit Gate is still asked on its own.
- **What the metrics cover.** Only delivered Vídeos with both dates count: in-progress numbers are not final. `medias` is `null` before the first delivery, not zeros. `rodadas` is the Vídeo's `rodada` field, which equals the number of the last version reviewed.
- **The learnings question is not counted** in `perguntas` or `gates`. It comes after the Entrega, and the metrics measure the load up to delivery.
- **No new `nextStep` action for Entregue Vídeos.** The existing test "a delivered Vídeo is finished… a new Vídeo comes next" requires `novo-video`. The new top-level `aprendizadosPendentes` field is offered in one line by the estudio skill instead.
- **Learning edits follow editar-kit's merge**: objects merge; lists are replaced whole. Approved learnings are merged in order.

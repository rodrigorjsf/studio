## What was built
- New CLI command `qc "<folder>" "<projeto>" "<vídeo>" "<render>" "<ffmpeg>" "<ffprobe>"` (`plugin/estudio/scripts/lib/qc.mjs`). It holds one render (path relative to the Vídeo folder) against the Master and returns:
  - `passed`;
  - `problemas` `[{check, message}]`;
  - `medidas` for the render and the Master (size, fps, duration, audio tracks, integrated loudness in LUFS, true peak in dBTP, black and frozen stretches);
  - `verificacaoManual`, which always holds `photosensitivity`.
- The checks: `duration` (±1 frame), `resolution` (a Formato canvas), `frame-rate` (constant 30 fps), `audio-tracks` (exactly 1), `loudness` (within 1.5 LU of the Master), `black-frames` (0.5 s or more) and `frozen-frames` (2 s or more). A stretch the Master already has is excused. Loudness and true peak are reported and never normalized.
- `entregar` now takes `"<ffmpeg>" "<ffprobe>"` and runs the same checks on every MP4, so a failed QC blocks the Entrega with its reasons. Its success answer also carries `verificacaoManual`.
- New Crítico persona `plugin/estudio/agents/qc-tecnico.md` (claude-sonnet-5-5, effort high, tools Bash and Read). In the `edicao` skill it judges the version's full render before her review opens, and the Finalizador's MP4s before the Diretor runs `entregar`.

## Decisions & deviations
- **Resolution** must be one of the three Formato canvases (1080×1920, 1920×1080, 1080×1080). It is not checked against the Master's size, because the Master may be recorded in another size, or against the Kit's Formato, because other Formatos can be delivered on request.
- **Frame rate** must be the edit's constant 30 fps (`FPS_DA_EDICAO` in the template), not the Master's rate: Remotion always renders at 30.
- **Loudness is a fail condition** when the render is more than 1.5 LU from the Master. This catches her voice doubled, dropped, or replaced by Remotion's silent track, and it is the same tolerance #11 used. True peak is only reported.
- **Loudness is measured as played in stereo.** A mono track is copied to both channels (`pan=stereo|c0=c0|c1=c0`), because Remotion renders a mono Master that way: a real render measured +3 LU over the mono Master. ffmpeg's own `-ac 2` upmix is equal-power and would be flagged, but only Remotion makes renders.
- **The `-70` LUFS gate floor counts as silence** (`null`). Silence on both sides passes; silence on one side fails.
- **Black and frozen stretches** that overlap the same kind of stretch in the Master are excused. A frozen stretch that is black is reported as black only.
- **Flow change: the Motion designer renders each version in full** into `revisao/vNN/<nome> <formato>.mp4`, so QC can run before she sees the stills. The cost is one full render per Rodada.
- **Flow change: the Finalizador only renders.** The QC técnico judges, then the Diretor runs `entregar`.
- **Overlays (MOV)** keep only the duration and no-audio checks. Black and frozen detection has no meaning on a transparent file.
- **Out of scope, left to #13:** the Status `QC interno` and the cap of 3 internal turns. They are marked pending in the skill.

## Learnings
- ebur128 measures mono 3 LU below Remotion's stereo render of it. Upmix mono with `pan` before measuring.
- ffmpeg `-ac 2` upmixes mono at equal power; Remotion copies it at unity gain.
- freezedetect also flags black stretches, and an unfinished freeze has no `freeze_end`.
- An `-r 25` re-encode shifts the duration by 80 ms. Use `-vf fps=25` for a clean frame-rate fixture.

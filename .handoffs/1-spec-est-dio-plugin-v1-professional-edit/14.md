## What was built
- **The untrimmed warning.** New CLI subcommand `pausas "<folder>" "<projeto>" "<vídeo>" "<ffprobe>"` (`plugin/estudio/scripts/lib/precorte.mjs`). It lists the Master's long pauses from `palavras.json` and the probed Master duration: silences of 1.5 s or more before the first word, between two words, or after the last word. It sets `semCorte: true` when they add up to 3 s or more.
- **The Diretor's step.** New step 6 of `/estudio:novo-video`, with the procedure in `plugin/estudio/skills/novo-video/references/pre-corte.md`:
  - it warns only on `semCorte: true`, or when she asks for the Pré-corte herself;
  - it offers the Pré-corte as a Gate whose default answer is "no", never auto-approved;
  - it spawns the Editor de pré-corte;
  - it shows her the proposed cuts, and she approves all, some, or none;
  - it runs `precorte`.
- **The Editor de pré-corte persona.** `plugin/estudio/agents/editor-de-pre-corte.md`, `claude-sonnet-5-5`, effort `high`, tools Bash/Read/Write. It proposes silêncio / repetição / tropeço cuts, always between words, in `<vídeo>/precorte/proposta.json` with `cortes` and `manter`.
- **The cut.** New CLI subcommand `precorte "<folder>" "<projeto>" "<vídeo>" '{"manter":[{"inicio","fim"}]}' "<ffmpeg>" "<ffprobe>"`:
  - it snaps each boundary to a frame and refuses a boundary inside a word;
  - it re-encodes the kept segments (H.264 + AAC) into `master/<stem>.mp4`;
  - it moves the transcript onto the Master's clock, keeping the Original's in `transcricao/original/`;
  - it writes `precorte/mapa.json` and sets `master` in `video.md`;
  - it returns the new duration and the segment map. The Original stays byte-identical.

## Decisions & deviations
- **`pausas` is a new subcommand** that the spec's CLI list does not name. The warning in story 42 needs a deterministic check, and this keeps it testable at Seam 1.
- **`pausas` needs `ffprobe`,** because the trailing silence needs the Master's duration. A missing transcript is reported before any probe.
- **The thresholds:** a long pause is ≥ 1.5 s, and a recording looks untrimmed when its long pauses total ≥ 3 s. These are judgement calls (`PAUSA_LONGA`, `SEM_CORTE`, exported from `precorte.mjs`).
- **The Pré-corte is allowed only in `Briefing`,** as Esteira step 3, before Planejamento. This is derived from the `STATUS_NAMES` order in `estado.mjs`. `master-locked` is returned when `master !== original` or the Status is past `Briefing`. There is no redo; the skill offers a new Vídeo from the same recording instead.
- **Boundaries snap to the nearest frame,** so picture and sound stay in step and the map is exact. After snapping, a boundary strictly inside a word (`s < t < e`) is refused (`cuts-a-word`).
- **Beyond "returns duration and segment map", `precorte` also:**
  - rewrites `palavras.json` on the Master's clock, because every Palavra-gatilho reads it;
  - rebuilds `transcript.md` sentence by sentence;
  - keeps the Original's transcript in `transcricao/original/`;
  - persists the map in `precorte/mapa.json`.

  ADR 0003 now records the transcript and the "before the Plano" parts.
- **The Zona do rosto is kept as is.** It is one area over every frame of the Original, which is a superset of the Master's frames.
- **The output is always `.mp4`** (H.264 CRF 18, AAC 192k, yuv420p, faststart), whatever the Original's container is.
- **`findVideo` in `video.mjs` is now exported** and reused.

## What was built
- The Criadora can link her own Páginas Notion to a Projeto (several) or to one Vídeo (one or more). Zero is valid.
- Links are asked in three places:
  - the Projeto Grilling (`novo-projeto`, a question after the catalogue);
  - `/estudio:editar-projeto` (new section "Páginas Notion": link, unlink, change);
  - the Vídeo briefing (`novo-video` step 4).
- Shared Diretor rules live in `plugin/estudio/skills/estudio/references/notion.md`:
  - connector detection (`notion-fetch`), a lay pt-BR connection path, or skip, so Notion never blocks;
  - only linked pages are read, sub-pages only with consent, no workspace search;
  - the Resumo template, shown to her;
  - Kit conflicts: "só neste vídeo" / "atualizar o Kit", or "manter o Kit" / "atualizar o Kit" at Projeto level;
  - the change check before the Plano.
- New CLI commands (`plugin/estudio/scripts/lib/notion.mjs`):
  - `vincular-notion`;
  - `resumo-notion` (writes a dated `notion/resumo.md`);
  - `conferir-notion` (flags pages edited after the Resumo; `perguntarAtualizar`).
- `estado` validates `notion/paginas.json` and `notion/resumo.md`, and reports `notion: {paginas, resumo}` for each Projeto and Vídeo.
- `plano` requires a Plano not yet approved to cite each Resumo by its date (`resumosNotion`). The Roteirista gets only the Resumo paths.
- `scripts/check-plugin.mjs` enforces read-only Notion: no Notion tool except `notion-fetch`, no Notion server, no persona with a Notion tool, and every persona declares `tools`.

## Decisions & deviations
- **Storage.** Links and the Resumo live in `notion/paginas.json` and `notion/resumo.md` in each Projeto or Vídeo folder. `layout.mjs` had reserved `notion/` for this.
  - The Resumo is markdown with frontmatter: `geradoEm`, `paginasLidas`, `subpaginasLidas`.
  - The link question is not a `projeto.md` section. A section would have made every existing Projeto `incompleto`.
- **Change detection compares dates.** The page's last-edited time, as the connector reports it, is compared with the Resumo's `geradoEm`.
  - A page mapped to `null` (read, but no date shown) asks for a refresh.
  - A page left out (connector down) asks nothing, so Notion never blocks.
- **Resumo states.** `sem-paginas`, `ausente`, `atualizado`, `desatualizado`. `desatualizado` means the links or the sub-page consent changed after the Resumo.
  - A Resumo must cover every linked page.
  - It must read the sub-pages of every page she allowed (`missing-subpages`).
- **An approved Plano is not re-flagged** when a Resumo is refreshed later.
- **Projeto-level conflicts** (no Vídeo in question) use "manter o Kit" / "atualizar o Kit". At Projeto creation there is no Kit yet: the Resumo only suggests answers, and she decides them.
- **Every persona must now declare a `tools` allowlist.** This is the deterministic proof that no subagent inherits her Notion connector.
- **URL rule:** only https on `notion.so`, `*.notion.so` or `*.notion.site`, with a non-empty path.

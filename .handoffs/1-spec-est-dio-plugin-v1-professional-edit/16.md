## What was built
- **Higgsedit Gate `aprovar-higgsedit`** in `plugin/estudio/scripts/lib/creditos.mjs`. It takes `{pedido, trecho, creditosEstimados, saldo}`.
  - Without her words it refuses with `no-request`.
  - `trecho` is `"video-inteiro"` or `{inicio, fim}` in seconds of her Master.
  - Its other refusals match the credit Gate: `not-nivel-2`, `plano-not-approved`, `insufficient-balance`, `below-spent`, `over-budget`.
  - It counts +1 Gate and writes `higgseditCreditosEstimados/Gastos/AprovadoEm` to `video.md`. It appends her request to `higgsedit/pedidos.json`.
- **Pre-spend check `gastar-higgsedit`**, which takes exactly `{arquivo, creditos, saldo}`. `arquivo` must be a video directly inside `higgsedit/`.
  - It applies the same ~20% stop, on the Higgsedit estimate (`higgseditParadoEm`). Only a new `aprovar-higgsedit` lifts that stop.
  - It appends each run, with its `trecho`, to `higgsedit/higgsedit.json`.
- `creditos.mjs` now handles two budgets through one shared `approve`/`spend` core. The Kit's `porVideo`/`porMes` limits count both budgets together.
- **New persona `agents/montador-higgsedit.md`** (claude-sonnet-5-5, high; tools Bash, Read and the two Higgsfield connector names).
  - For a stretch, it saves the clip for the Motion designer to place with `<Gerado>` (muted).
  - For the whole Vídeo, it puts its render and stills in the version folder. The Finalizador copies that render into `entrega/`.
- **Skills and references:** `edicao` (the explicit-request procedure, its Gate and the same Críticos), `estudio` (the approval points), `level-2-higgsfield.md`, `getting-started.md`, the Motion designer and the Finalizador. The wiki is synced.

## Decisions & deviations
- **New persona "Montador Higgsedit"**, not listed in the spec's cast. #15 had explicitly barred the Artista generativo from Higgsedit and `sandbox_exec`, and the Motion designer has no Higgsfield tools.
- **The Kit budget covers both budgets.** The generation credits and Higgsedit count together against `porVideo`/`porMes`. This slightly changes `aprovar-creditos`: its refusal adds `aprovadosNoVideo`, and its answer adds `aprovadoEm`.
- **A whole-Vídeo Higgsedit montage** is delivered in the Kit's Formato only, with no other Formatos or overlays. The Diretor tells her this at the Gate.
- **The Higgsedit estimate** comes from the connector's quote, and Higgsedit does not run without one. Whether that quote is accurate is not verified. Check it in #19.
- **The Higgsedit request is stored in `higgsedit/pedidos.json`**, not in the frontmatter, because free text is unsafe there.

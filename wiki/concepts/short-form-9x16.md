# Short-form 9:16 rules

Primary format for the studio: **9:16 at 1080×1920** (Reels, TikTok, Shorts); 16:9 is a secondary composition. Split screens stack top/bottom.

- Platform-backed: proposition in the first 3 s; design for sound-off with on-screen text; motion and pace (TikTok ad guidance). Shorts up to 3 min (YouTube Help). Instagram ranks on watch time, sends and likes per reach; Trial Reels for hook A/B.
- Community-measured: safe box ~900×1400 centered plus a right margin for the icon column (84–120 px). Guide 5 sets ~250 px top / ~420 px bottom but no right margin — a gap. Reels profile grid crops to 3:4 (central 1080×1440 for cover text).
- Loudness: only YouTube ~−14 LUFS turn-down is documented; IG/TikTok figures are folklore.
- Folklore (label as such): jump cuts every 2–3 s, punch-in on every emphasis, word-by-word captions.

> **Contradiction:** repo `CLAUDE.md` cardápio lists 16:9 first; project decision (2026-09-29) makes 9:16 primary.

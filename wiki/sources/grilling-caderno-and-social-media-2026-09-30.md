# Grilling record — Caderno and Social media (2026-09-30)

Seventeen decisions (planned, not built) for two additions raised during spec #29 and kept out of its scope: a feedback memory (the Caderno) with issue drafting on `rodrigorjsf/studio`, and a new persona, the Social media, that writes the Texto do post. Raw: [2026-09-30-caderno-and-social-media.md](../../raw/grilling/2026-09-30-caderno-and-social-media.md). Recorded in `CONTEXT.md`, ADR 0007 and ADR 0008.

- Caderno: Estúdio and Projeto layers; Elogios / Queixas / Soluções; any persona originates, only the Diretor writes; conflicts resolved by her; never changes the Kit.
- Issues: potential bugs and self-evolution proposals become sanitized Rascunhos de issue, published only after her Gate at the close, never automatic.
- Social media: runs beside the Finalizador, writes `entrega/texto-do-post.md` for the Kit's new `plataformas`, no Gate, judged by the Revisor de plataforma on official rules only.

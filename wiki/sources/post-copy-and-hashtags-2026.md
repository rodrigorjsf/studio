# Research — post copy and hashtags (2026)

Web research (2026-09-30, by a subagent, not re-run in the main thread) on what the post text, the Shorts title and hashtags do for distribution on Instagram Reels, TikTok and YouTube Shorts, for a pt-BR creator. Raw: [findings.md](../../raw/research/post-copy-and-hashtags-2026/findings.md).

## Key claims

- Distribution follows viewer behaviour (watch time, completion, likes, sends, swipe-away); text mainly helps classification and search [sourced — TikTok newsroom 2020, Mosseri 2025, YouTube 2023].
- Keywords in the caption beat hashtags for Instagram search ("by far the most important signal" is the typed query) [sourced — Instagram 2021-08-25].
- Instagram caps posts and Reels at 5 hashtags since 2025-12-18 [sourced — @creators]; Mosseri: hashtags do not raise reach [sourced, via press].
- YouTube ignores every hashtag on a video with more than 60; up to 3 "most engaging" show by the title, not the first 3 [sourced — YouTube Help].
- `#fyp`/`#foryou`/`#viral`/`#explore`: no platform states a benefit [sourced]; "TikTok confirmed it does nothing" has no primary source [unverified].
- Engagement bait is excluded from recommendations on Instagram and TikTok and is spam on YouTube [sourced].
- Visible-before-cutoff lengths, TikTok hashtag counts and caption-length stats are marketing claims [unverified].
- Nothing Brazil-specific found; pt-BR keywords are an inference.

# Overview

The studio repo (fork of mackswendhell/studio) is being turned into a Claude Code / Claude Desktop plugin that simulates a professional video-editing studio for small, part-time creators ([PROJETO.md](../PROJETO.md); dominant risk: solution). Vertical 9:16 is primary; 16:9 is accepted.

Current synthesis ([research report](sources/estudio-profissional-de-video-report.md)): the master is already picture-locked, so the studio's work is composition on top and the creative loop is brief → plan → style stills ([picture-lock](concepts/picture-lock.md)). What the repo lacks is a persisted brief ([grilling-catalogue](concepts/grilling-catalogue.md)), a persisted and enforced [brand kit per front](concepts/brand-kit-per-front.md), and a critic that is not the maker ([qc-technical-vs-editorial](concepts/qc-technical-vs-editorial.md), [studio-personas](entities/studio-personas.md)). Gates share one [approval-gate](concepts/approval-gate.md) shape and run on the main thread of a director entry skill ([plugin-architecture](concepts/plugin-architecture.md)). Success metric to test first: fewest questions per video once a front's kit exists.

Design settled in the 2026-09-29 grilling ([record](sources/grilling-estudio-plugin-2026-09-29.md), [settled design](analyses/estudio-plugin-decisions.md)): plugin `estudio`, Perfil → Projetos → Vídeos, 12 personas with pinned model/effort, both Níveis with Remotion montage, optional Pré-corte, no-admin installer. Glossary in [CONTEXT.md](../CONTEXT.md); decisions in ADRs 0001–0005.

Built so far: the plugin skeleton and marketplace (ticket #2), the Estúdio folder with the `estado`/`criar` CLI (ticket #3), the session-start check plus the no-admin installer (ticket #4), and the Perfil interview with Autonomia, `/estudio:perfil` (ticket #5), all in [plugin-architecture](concepts/plugin-architecture.md); and the Projeto Grilling with a validated Kit de marca behind an approval Gate (ticket #6), then the Projeto list and Kit edits that leave started Vídeos on the Kit they began with (ticket #7), and the Remotion template's compositions that read the Kit for colors, fonts, captions, motion and Formato, 9:16 by default with 16:9 on request (ticket #8), all three in [brand-kit-per-front](concepts/brand-kit-per-front.md). The plugin is self-contained (ticket #23): its own frame sampler (vendored MIT code) and Remotion rules (distilled) replace the external `watch` and `remotion-best-practices` skills (`plugin/estudio/THIRD_PARTY.md`, `plugin/estudio/vendor.json`). A new Vídeo (ticket #9) stores the Original untouched, asks only the Kit's gaps plus the Nível, and is ingested by the Assistente de edição: word-timed transcript and a Zona do rosto measured on every frame of the whole clip ([plugin-architecture](concepts/plugin-architecture.md)). Its Plano (ticket #10) is checked word by word against the transcript, the face and the Área livre, shown with 2–3 Quadros de estilo on real frames, and approved at one Gate when the Kit defines the style, two otherwise ([plugin-architecture](concepts/plugin-architecture.md)). The optional Pré-corte (ticket #14) warns when a recording looks untrimmed, lets the Editor de pré-corte propose cuts between words for her approval, and writes the approved cut as a new Master while the Original stays byte-identical ([plugin-architecture](concepts/plugin-architecture.md), [picture-lock](concepts/picture-lock.md)). The edit (ticket #11) is built on her Master as one continuous layer, reviewed on key stills in counted Rodadas (v01, v02…; three decisions, notes consolidated, scope changes named) and delivered into the Vídeo's `entrega/` folder only when every file lasts as long as the Master with her audio once; the Diretor then opens the folder ([plugin-architecture](concepts/plugin-architecture.md), [approval-gate](concepts/approval-gate.md)). The technical QC (ticket #12) holds each render against the Master — duration, a Formato's resolution, 30 fps, her audio once at the Master's loudness (loudness and true peak reported, never normalized), no new black or frozen stretches, photosensitivity left to a person — run by the QC técnico Crítico before she sees the stills and before delivery; a failed QC blocks the Entrega ([plugin-architecture](concepts/plugin-architecture.md)). The brand and platform Críticos (ticket #13) join the QC técnico: before she sees a version (and the Guardião and the Revisor before she sees the Quadros), the three judge it, the Motion designer fixes what they reject without bothering her, and after three rejected turns the Diretor asks her one sentence with two options; each turn and its tokens and seconds are recorded in the Vídeo document ([plugin-architecture](concepts/plugin-architecture.md), [approval-gate](concepts/approval-gate.md)). Read-only Notion context (ticket #20): she links Páginas Notion to a Projeto or a Vídeo. The Diretor reads only those pages through her own connector and writes a dated Resumo Notion. The Plano cites that Resumo, a page changed since the Resumo triggers a refresh question before the Plano, and the structure check forbids any Notion tool other than the page reader ([notion-context](concepts/notion-context.md)). Nível 2 (ticket #15) adds Higgsfield images and clips through her own connector, behind a credit Gate that checks her balance and the Kit's budget. Each generation is cleared before it is paid: its prompt must forbid text, and the spending stops at the estimate plus ~20%. The assets are saved in the Vídeo's `gerados/` folder and assembled in Remotion ([plugin-architecture](concepts/plugin-architecture.md), [approval-gate](concepts/approval-gate.md)). Higgsedit (ticket #16) montages a stretch or the whole Vídeo only on her explicit request, behind its own cost Gate and ~20% stop, run by the Montador Higgsedit; the result passes the same QC and Críticos ([plugin-architecture](concepts/plugin-architecture.md)). Autonomia, metrics and Kit learnings (ticket #17): with Autonomia alta the Diretor approves the Plano and Quadros Gates for her, recorded and told, never credits, the Pré-corte, the Kit or her review; `estado` reports per Projeto the averages of questions, Gates, Rodadas and minutes to delivery; after the Entrega she approves which learnings go into the Kit, and the Vídeo is archived ([plugin-architecture](concepts/plugin-architecture.md), [brand-kit-per-front](concepts/brand-kit-per-front.md)).

Repo cleanup (ticket #18): the README is now the Criadora-facing pt-BR hub with the marketplace install guide, the maintainer guide is `docs/developer-guide.md`, every persona declares a display color, and the structure check rejects upstream leftovers in the package ([plugin architecture](concepts/plugin-architecture.md)). Ticket #30 finished it: the root `CLAUDE.md` is a maintainer guide and the upstream root folders are gone.


Offline transcription (spec #29, ADR 0006): the speech model (`large-v3-turbo`, ~1.6 GB) is mirrored on this repository's own GitHub Release by the maintainer script `scripts/espelhar-modelo.sh`, always from a fresh download of the pinned upstream, and recorded with each file's sha256 in `plugin/estudio/vendor.json` (ticket #31; the Release itself is not published yet). The Preparação's `modelo` step downloads it once into the plugin data folder, verifies every sha256, continues a file cut halfway on the next run and prints a progress line while it downloads; the check reports `speech-model` as missing, and `transcrever.py` loads the model by path, never reaches Hugging Face, and exits 3 when the model is not prepared (ticket #32, review round 1). `plugin/estudio/hosts.json` lists every host the Preparação reaches, with no Hugging Face host; `scripts/check-plugin.mjs` fails on an installer host the list lacks, and the Diretor gives the allowlist guidance only after a blocked download (ticket #33). The Preparação and check scripts use English identifiers, glossary terms aside (ticket #35) ([plugin-architecture](concepts/plugin-architecture.md)).

Esteira documentation (ticket #36): the README section "Quem trabalha em cada etapa" is the single source for every actor (who calls it, what it hands back, what it does on approval and rejection), the top-down call-chain diagram and the Gates table; the wiki links to it instead of repeating it ([studio-personas](entities/studio-personas.md)).

Kit `plataformas` (ticket #41, spec #39): the Kit records which of Reels, TikTok and Shorts the Projeto posts on (absent = all three), asked in the Projeto Grilling and changed by `editar-kit` ([brand-kit-per-front](concepts/brand-kit-per-front.md)).

Planned next (grilling 2026-09-30, ADRs 0007–0008): a two-layer [Caderno](concepts/caderno.md) of Elogios, Queixas and Soluções that only the Diretor writes, with sanitized issue drafts published on her approval; and a Social media persona writing the [Texto do post](concepts/post-copy.md) from bundled, dated platform rules ([research](sources/post-copy-and-hashtags-2026.md)).
